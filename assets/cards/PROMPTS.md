# Yume · Nine illustrated feature backgrounds

Nove concept pensati per le nove card della home. Sono stati **realizzati e pubblicati come SVG originali** (non collegamenti a immagini inesistenti). I testi sotto sono prompt di riferimento per un'eventuale futura rigenerazione illustrata in AI.

**Stile comune obbligatorio:** hand-crafted Japanese-inspired editorial anime illustration, subtle watercolor and clean vector silhouettes, wistful dreamlike atmosphere, elegant soft glow, midnight navy and violet with sakura pink and pale turquoise accents, sophisticated graphic composition, gentle artistic grain, 16:8 landscape, right-side focal composition, deliberately dark low-detail left half for readable Italian text, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no photorealistic people, no sexualization, cohesive with the Yume manga/anime website.
